# Mohamed & Mariem: Better Together

A 3D co-op love story game in the spirit of *It Takes Two*, made for Mohamed and Mariem. Nine chapters follow their story, from a glance across a classroom in 2021 to the wedding and the family they dream of. Every chapter is built around the two of them working together. Neither of you can finish a chapter alone.

## Play it

**Same computer, right now:** download or clone this repository and open `index.html` in Chrome, Edge or Safari. No install, no build step. Same-screen co-op and solo work straight from the file.

**Online (two phones or laptops):** the game needs to be on a website for online play. The free way is GitHub Pages:

1. In this repository on GitHub, open **Settings → Pages**.
2. Under **Build and deployment**, choose **Deploy from a branch**. Pick the branch with the game and the `/ (root)` folder, then **Save**.
3. After a minute the game is live at `https://mohammadyousry.github.io/-------/`.

Any static host works too (Vercel, Netlify, Cloudflare Pages): upload the folder as-is.

Online play uses a direct peer-to-peer connection (WebRTC through PeerJS's free servers). It works best on the same Wi-Fi and usually works across different networks too. One player picks **Play online → Create room**, the other enters the 5-letter code or opens the invite link.

## Modes

| Mode | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Play together** | One screen, split down the middle (or a shared camera). Share one keyboard, or use gamepads. |
| **Play online** | Each of you on your own device, each with your own camera. The host picks the chapter. |
| **Play solo** | Control both. `Tab` switches character; `C` calls your partner over when there's a clear path. |

## Controls

| | Player 1 | Player 2 | Gamepad |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Move | `W A S D` | Arrow keys | Left stick / D-pad |
| Jump | `Space` | `Enter`, `.` or Numpad `0` | `A` |
| Power | `E` | Right `Shift`, `/` or Numpad `1` | `X` or `B` |
| Love (hug when close) | `Q` | `,` or Numpad `2` | `Y` |
| Pause | `Esc` / `P` | | `Start` |

With one gamepad connected, it controls Player 2 so Player 1 keeps the keyboard. Phones get an on-screen stick and buttons.

## Two kinds of power

**Mohamed, the big guy:** pushes heavy boxes and furniture, is the only one heavy enough for the gold buttons, **tosses** Mariem up to high ledges (Power next to her) and **ground-slams** through cracked walls (Jump, then Power). Mariem can ride on his shoulders.

**Mariem, the little kitten:** **double jumps**, **dashes** across wide gaps (even in mid-air), runs faster and fits through tunnels he never will.

## The chapters

1. **2021 · First Glance**: the classroom where it started. Doors, tunnels, a toss and a final exam you pass together.
2. **2022 · Zero to One**: zero money, one dream. Smash the walls of doubt, collect skill books, reach the first paycheck.
3. **2023 · Miles Apart**: he studies in the United States, she's in Cairo. Two lanes, one ocean; her buttons build his bridges and his weights open her doors, until the call connects.
4. **2024 · U3 AI**: back in Egypt, building the agency. Bring three servers online and launch.
5. **2025 · The Nile Night**: the boat restaurant with blue velvet chairs and warm lamps. Light the candles, find the ring, meet at the table.
6. **2026 · The Open Road**: our car. He steers, she runs the radio; notes only count on the matching station.
7. **2026 · Our Home**: move the furniture, find the decor, turn on the lights together.
8. **December 2026 · Dr. Mariem**: coffee runs and the graduation stage.
9. **Forever · Our Future**: passport stamps across Egypt, Paris, the Maldives and the States, then the wedding arch, and a little girl and boy.

## Make it even more yours

- **Our album:** every chapter hides memories. Unlock them, then add your own photos in **Our album**. Photos stay on the device (they're never uploaded) and also appear in the 3D photo frames inside the game.
- **Our song:** **Settings → Our song** plays any MP3 instead of the built-in music.
- **Outfits:** **Settings** has both outfits from your photos, *Nile night* and *Café date*.
- Progress saves automatically on each device.

## Under the hood

- Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with [three.js](https://threejs.org) r160 and [PeerJS](https://peerjs.com) 1.5.4 vendored in `vendor/` (both MIT). No build step.
- All characters, props, music and sound effects are generated in code. There are no image, model or audio files.
- `src/levels.js` holds the story: themes, chapters, puzzles and memories. Each level is built from simple pieces (`L.plate`, `L.door`, `L.crate`, `L.elevator`, `L.bridge`, `L.mover`, `L.breakable`, `L.goalZone`...), so it's easy to add a chapter.
- Open the game with `?debug=1` for an FPS readout; press `N` to finish a chapter instantly.
- `tests/` has bot playthroughs that drive both characters through every chapter's puzzles with the real physics, so a layout change that breaks a puzzle shows up as a failure. Run them with `npm install --no-save playwright && npx playwright install chromium && node tests/run.js` (or `node tests/run.js 3 5` for specific chapters).

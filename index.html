    How to play

    Two players, two kinds of power

    Mohamed · the big guy

    • Strong: pushes heavy boxes and furniture.
    • Heavy: only he can press the big gold buttons.
    • Toss (Power near Mariem): throws her up to high ledges.
    • Ground Slam (Jump, then Power): smashes cracked blocks.
    • Mariem can ride on his shoulders. Literally.

    Mariem · the little kitten

    • Kitten Leap: jump again in mid-air.
    • Kitten Dash (Power): zips across wide gaps, even in the air.
    • Small: fits through tunnels he never will.
    • Light on her feet: faster than him, too.

    Together

    • Pink buttons open things while someone stands on them.
    • Collect hearts and find memories hidden in every chapter.
    • Finish a chapter by standing in the glowing heart zone, both of you.
    • Love (Q) next to each other: hug.
    • Stuck? Pause → Regroup or Skip chapter.

    Controls

    Player 1: WASD move, Space jump, E power, Q love.
    Player 2: Arrows move, Enter or . jump, R-Shift or / power, , love. Numpad 012 work too.
    Solo: Tab switches character, C calls your partner to you.
    Gamepad: stick, A jump, X/B power, Y love, Start pause.
    Pause: Esc or P.
